About

I work where business context, data systems, and practical change meet.

I am a data, analytics, and BI professional who enjoys turning unclear business questions into systems people can actually use. My work moves between stakeholder discovery, data modeling, reporting design, performance improvement, workflow automation, and practical AI-enabled support.

How I think

From signals to priorities and action.

I bring fragmented inputs together, establish the decision that matters, and shape a focused path from evidence to action.

SignalsPrioritiesAction

A useful system should make the next decision easier.

I care about the details that determine whether a reporting or automation effort succeeds: a clear definition of the problem, trustworthy metric logic, thoughtful data design, an experience that matches how people work, and enough documentation for the solution to remain useful over time.

I am comfortable working with both business stakeholders and technical teams. That means moving from a question about performance, cost, adoption, or operations to the data, system design, and delivery choices required to answer it.

Strength areas

Business intelligence and decision support

KPI design, executive reporting, dashboard UX, semantic models, and reporting systems built for action.

Analytics engineering and data platforms

SQL, data modeling, Snowflake, Microsoft Fabric, Databricks, performance improvement, and governed data workflows.

Automation and AI solutions

Workflow automation, practical exception handling, AI-enabled decision support, and implementation choices grounded in process and data readiness.

Consulting and delivery

Requirement discovery, stakeholder alignment, technical translation, documentation, enablement, and continuous improvement.

Working principles

  • Start with the decision, not the tool.
  • Make metric logic clear enough to trust.
  • Prefer reusable systems over one-off fixes.
  • Automate repetitive work thoughtfully.
  • Use AI where it improves a real workflow, not because it is fashionable.
  • Make technical work understandable to the people who depend on it.
